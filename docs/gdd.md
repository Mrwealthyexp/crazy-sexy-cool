# Game Design Document - Crazy Sexy Cool

## Core Loop
1. Enter the world with a wallet identity.
2. Forge a single soul with a name, temperament, birth month, and token-bound vault.
3. Build focus and white karma through meditation and creation.
4. Convert skill into COOL by completing bounties.
5. Unlock regulated combat through a karmic licensing gate.
6. Reach transcendence thresholds and author the world's collective question.

## Implemented Systems

### Soul Forge
- One soul per wallet.
- Tracks display identity, temperament, birth month, world state, focus, combat license, and transcendence state.

### Karmic Ledger
- **White karma** represents generous or restorative actions.
- **Gray karma** represents productive but self-interested gains.
- **Black karma** represents reckless force.
- Reputation formula: `white * 2 + gray - black * 3`.

### Reward Economy
- Forge soul: **100 COOL**
- Create artifact: **15 COOL**
- Complete bounty: **mastery score in COOL**
- Licensed combat: **5–12 COOL** depending on conduct
- Transcendence: **250 COOL**

### Combat and Regulation
- Combat is only legal in **Shadow Arena**.
- A combat license requires **25 focus** and **5 reputation**.
- Honorable combat increases white karma; reckless combat increases black karma.

### Transcendence
- Requires combat license, **100 focus**, **20 white karma**, **black karma <= 5**, and **25 reputation**.
- Unlocks a world question that changes shared reality for 24 hours.

## World Zones
- **Soul Forge**: origin point for identity minting.
- **Aeterna Gate**: high-value creation district.
- **Shadow Arena**: regulated combat territory.
- **Oracle District**: meditation and world-question territory.
