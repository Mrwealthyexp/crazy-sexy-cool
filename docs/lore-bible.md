# Lore Bible - Crazy Sexy Cool

## Premise

By 2075, climate reversal and civil collapse have redrawn the world. Antarctica has opened into Aeterna, a monumental city layered with forgotten technology, sacred geometry, and contested social power.

## World Tone

Crazy Sexy Cool is glamorous, dangerous, spiritual, and theatrical. Its societies treat status, style, territory, and reputation as public ritual.

## Core Locations

### Aeterna Core
A fortified civic zone where trade, governance, and public ceremony happen under heavy surveillance and strict noncombat rules.

### The Gilded Galleries
Luxury districts where creators, collectors, and cultural houses shape taste, prestige, and soft power.

### Tidebreak Docks
A dense logistics frontier connecting off-world imports, black-market brokers, and fast-moving social factions.

### The Shadow Belts
Outer combat districts where licensed fighters compete for rank, influence, and territorial leverage.

## Factions

### House Cool
Values discipline, distance, and strategic control.

### House Sexy
Values desire, beauty, and influence through culture.

### House Crazy
Values risk, disruption, and forceful change.

## Social Order

Identity is visible, status is earned, and reputation can unlock or restrict access. Combat is not universal; it is regulated, observed, and politically consequential.
