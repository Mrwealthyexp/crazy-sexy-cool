# Lore Bible — Crazy Sexy Cool

## Core Cosmology

The world of Crazy Sexy Cool teaches that every being begins in attachment. No one enters pure. No one enters finished. A soul must pass through distortion before it can wield truth without violence.

The city of **Aeterna** is both playground and monastery: a megacity revealed after the thawing of Antarctica, ringed by impossible walls and statues so large they seem to judge the horizon itself.

The city is alive. It responds to motive. It remembers the emotional truth of conflict. It rewards mystery more than fame.

## Sacred Law

Three laws govern the world:

1. **Birth shapes entry, not destiny**  
   A soul’s blueprint determines pressure points, not final worth.

2. **Power follows karma**  
   Strength without moral alignment collapses into debt.

3. **Intervention must teach**  
   Miracles that serve ego eventually strip the miracle-worker of power.

## The Three Worldly Attachments

### Crazy
The fire of reaction. Anger, pride, speed, hunger, domination.

### Sexy
The water of longing. Desire, beauty, attention, intimacy, appetite.

### Cool
The wind of detachment. Intelligence, style, irony, distance, control.

These states are not evil. They are incomplete. The world asks the player to know them so deeply that transcendence becomes honest rather than performative.

## The Great Teacher

A Great Teacher is not a ruler in the political sense. They are closer to a weather pattern with agency. Their role is to create second chances, true endings, and moral clarity where ordinary systems fail.

Others often do not know when a Great Teacher has acted. This is intentional. Anonymous grace is considered more spiritually advanced than visible heroism.

## Weather as Theology

Weather in CSC is not cosmetic. It is a sermon.

- **Rain** means confession and emotional release.
- **Sunbreak** means revelation.
- **Storm** means purification through ordeal.
- **Snow** means stillness beyond violence.
- **Rainbow** means miracle and restored possibility.
- **Dark** means intuition without certainty.

## Dreams and Mystery

When a player is saved by divine intervention, they are not handed a system notification. They are given:

- a symbol
- a dream
- a compulsion to seek meaning

The search for the source is itself the teaching.

## The World Question

At the highest level of play, an Enlightened presence may ask the server a single question. Everyone must answer. Reality bends around the collective response.

This means lore is not static. It becomes participatory theology.

## Tone

The tone of Crazy Sexy Cool should always hold these tensions together:

- mystical but legible
- luxurious but dangerous
- intimate but cosmic
- seductive but ethical
- stylish but spiritually serious
